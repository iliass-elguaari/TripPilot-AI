*Sample run: Italy · 3 days · Luxury · Relaxing*

**Day 1: Welcome to Rome**

*Morning:*
- **Arrival and Check-in:** Arrive at Leonardo da Vinci International Airport (FCO) and be greeted by a private chauffeur who will take you to the luxurious **Hassler Roma Hotel**. Located at the top of the Spanish Steps, it offers a magnificent base in the heart of Rome.
- **Leisurely Breakfast:** Enjoy a sumptuous breakfast at **Imàgo**, the hotel's Michelin-starred restaurant offering panoramic views of the city.

*Afternoon:*
- **Relaxing Spa Session:** After settling in, head to the hotel's exclusive spa for a rejuvenating treatment that will prepare you for your Roman adventure.
- **Stroll in Villa Borghese Gardens:** Take a leisurely stroll through the nearby Villa Borghese Gardens, an oasis of peace and beauty with opportunities to relax on a bench or enjoy an unhurried visit to the Borghese Gallery.

*Evening:*
- **Dinner at La Pergola:** Your evening will culminate with a rhetorical dining experience at **La Pergola**, a three-Michelin-starred restaurant known for its exceptional Mediterranean cuisine and impeccable service.

---

**Day 2: Discovering Florence**

*Morning:*
- **High-speed Train to Florence:** After breakfast, embark on a first-class journey to Florence via the Frecciarossa train.
- **Check-in:** Arrive at **Hotel Savoy, A Rocco Forte Hotel** in the heart of Florence. Enjoy a welcome drink upon arrival.

*Afternoon:*
- **Private Art and Architecture Tour:** Spend your afternoon with a guide exploring key cultural sites leisurely, such as the Florence Cathedral, Baptistery, and the Uffizi Gallery.
- **Lunch at Enoteca Pinchiorri:** Enjoy an elegant lunch showcasing contemporary Italian cuisine, accompanied by exquisite wines from their renowned cellar.

*Evening:*
- **Relax at Piazzale Michelangelo:** Conclude your day with a serene sunset view over Florence from Piazzale Michelangelo.
- **Dinner at Cibrèo Ristorante:** Experience traditional Tuscan fare with a modern twist at Cibrèo, a restaurant known for its warm atmosphere and attention to culinary detail.

---

**Day 3: Serenity in Tuscany's Countryside**

*Morning:*
- **Private Transfer to Tuscany:** After a lavish breakfast, take a chauffeured ride into the serene Tuscan countryside.
- **Exploration of Chianti Region:** Enjoy a tour of the famous Chianti vineyards, complete with a wine-tasting session at the prestigious **Antinori nel Chianti Classico** estate.

*Afternoon:*
- **Lunch at Osteria di Passignano:** Delight in a laid-back lunch at this Michelin-starred restaurant amidst vineyards and olive groves.
- **Relaxing Afternoon:** Spend a tranquil afternoon lounging by the vineyards or take a leisurely walk through the estate's picturesque landscape.

*Evening:*
- **Return to Florence:** Head back to Florence for a restful night.
- **Farewell Dinner at Il Palagio:** Celebrate your last night in Italy with a special dinner at **Il Palagio**, located in the Four Seasons Hotel, renowned for its sophisticated ambiance and culinary excellence.

---

Throughout this itinerary, you'll enjoy a blend of cultural exploration, culinary delights, and indulgent relaxation, ensuring a memorable luxury trip through some of Italy's most beautiful locations. Each experience has been carefully selected to offer a sense of tranquility and opulence, leaving you rejuvenated and inspired.
